Island Dog is founded by Laura Schutz and inspired by her two French bulldogs named Turks and Caicos.

The Island Dog Promise

At Island Dog we promise to only carry brands that share the same values and interests as us and our customers.

As a small boutique we only carry specific brands that we know will benefit your dogs health and wellbeing. We are here to help your dog feel good, look good, and get more out of life!

Our Core Values

Community

Giving back to our community and helping dogs-in-need is one of the reasons why Island Dog was created. We are very passionate about working with local shelters and will make it a priority to support dogs in need.

Innovation

Island Dog understands that the "pet industry" especially in Edmonton is... well lacking. We promise to always: keep it stylish, new and fun!

Inclusion

At Island Dog we understand that there are many different types of dogs and dog parents. When the dog community comes together the world is a beautiful place. We will always treat our community with kindness and respect.

Authenticity

We are not afraid to be unique and different at Island Dog. We don't stock anything that we wouldn't or haven't used with our own dogs.
