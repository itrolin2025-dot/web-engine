BARF stands for Biologically Appropriate Raw Food. It is a natural diet consisting of raw meat, bones, vegetables, and fruit designed to mimic what dogs naturally eat in the wild.

A BARF diet provides better digestion, healthier skin, a shinier coat, cleaner teeth, more energy, and smaller, less odorous stools compared to traditional kibble.

Key benefits include improved immunity, natural oral hygiene, optimal weight management, and reduced risk of allergies caused by artificial preservatives.

Dogs have a shorter, highly acidic digestive tract designed to process raw meat safely. However, proper hygiene and handling procedures should always be followed when preparing meals.

Keep the meals frozen at -18°C. Thaw the portion you need in the refrigerator 24 hours before feeding. Once thawed, use it within 2–3 days.

Generally, adult dogs need 2% to 3% of their ideal body weight daily. Puppies and active dogs may require higher proportions (up to 5%-10%).
